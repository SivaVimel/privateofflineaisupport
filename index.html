Support

The whole app runs on your phone, so most things are fixable right there. Start here — and if it is not covered, email sivavimelrajhen@gmail.com with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Getting started

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. The AI model ships inside the app. Install it, open it, and start typing — in Airplane Mode if you like. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to download on first launch.

Why is the app so large?

About 800 MB of the install is the language model itself. That is the trade for an assistant that works with no connection and sends nothing anywhere. Download over Wi-Fi.

The first reply is slow

The model is loaded into memory the first time you send something after launch. After that, replies start quickly. On older iPhones the app automatically uses a lighter configuration, which is slower but uses far less memory.

Chatting

Give it a standing instruction

+ → Instruction. Anything you put there — "always answer in bullet points", "reply in Malay" — is applied to every new reply until you change it.

Ask about an image, a photo or a document

Use + in the message bar: Image for a photo you already have, Camera to take one, File for a PDF, Word, Excel or text document. Ask your question in the same message. Text recognition runs on the device; nothing is uploaded.

Talk instead of typing

Tap the microphone. Speech recognition uses iOS and needs Microphone and Speech Recognition permission the first time.

Start a fresh conversation / find an old one

The clock icon in the header lists your recent conversations; the + button there starts a clean one. Tap any conversation to pick it back up.

Going online (all optional)

The app is offline by default

Nothing below happens unless you turn it on, and turning it off stops it. The privacy policy sets out exactly what each one sends and to whom.

Web search

Tap the globe in the message bar to let a reply draw on live results. The chips — News, Location, Images, Videos — narrow what it looks for. Tap the globe again to go back offline.

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another provider instead

AI button → Import your AI API. Paste your own key for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek or Qwen, or point Custom at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — including a local server such as Ollama or LM Studio on your own network. You are billed by that provider, not by us. Switch back to the on-device model from the same menu at any time.

Use a different local model

AI button → Import your AI model and pick a .gguf file from Files. The bundled Llama 3.2 1B cannot be deleted, so you always have a working fallback.

Mail

InApp → Mail. Choose your provider and connect. Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo and iCloud all require an app-specific password rather than your normal one — the app shows the steps for each. The app talks to your mail server directly; nothing passes through us.

If connecting fails, it is almost always the password type. Generate a fresh app password and paste it without spaces.

Appearance

Change the theme colour

Sliders icon in the header → Settings. Eight accent colours; the whole app — logo, chat bubbles, buttons and menus — follows immediately. Light and dark mode are in the same place. The Home Screen icon keeps its own colour.

Your data

Where is it, and how do I get it out?

Everything is in the app's own folder on your iPhone. Open the Files app → On My iPhone → Private Offline AI to copy conversations, notes and saved images out. Individual replies have Copy and Share buttons underneath them.

Moving to a new phone

An encrypted iPhone backup (iCloud or Finder) carries the app's data across. There is no account to sign into, so nothing syncs by itself — if you restore from a backup you keep your conversations; if you install fresh, you start clean.

Deleting everything

Delete the app. Nothing of yours exists anywhere else, because nothing is ever sent anywhere.

Troubleshooting

"Model file not found in app bundle"

The install is incomplete. Delete the app and reinstall it over Wi-Fi with enough free space — you need roughly 1 GB free.

The app closes while generating a long answer

On 3 GB and 4 GB iPhones, memory is tight. Close other apps, start a new conversation rather than continuing a very long one, and avoid attaching several large documents at once.

Web search returns nothing

Check the globe is lit and that you have a connection. Some networks and VPNs block the search providers the app uses.

Replies are wrong or made up

The bundled model is a small one — about a billion parameters — chosen so it fits and runs on a phone. It is good at drafting, rewriting, summarising and everyday questions, and it will sometimes get facts wrong. For anything that matters, check it. For harder work, connect a larger cloud model with your own key.
